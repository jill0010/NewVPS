FAQ

Domain Questions

01Do I need to provide my real identity?

No. Domains are tied to your AnonPort account token (no name, no email, no phone number). WHOIS privacy is enabled by default — public WHOIS shows the registrar's privacy proxy, never your personal data.

02Which TLDs do you support?

The most popular gTLDs (.com .net .org .io .me .co .xyz .app .dev .ai) plus privacy-friendly ccTLDs (.fr .de .nl .ch .li .is .pw .uk). We deliberately do not offer .eu .es .it .ca — the registry itself requires identity verification, which would break our no-KYC promise.

03How is this different from Njalla?

Same privacy outcome, different model: at AnonPort you are the legal registrant (with WHOIS proxying your data), whereas Njalla holds the domain on your behalf. We're cheaper, instant, and let you transfer out at any time without negotiation.

04Which cryptocurrencies do you accept?

BTC, XMR, ETH, USDT (ERC-20/TRC-20), SOL, LTC, TRX — 8 in total. Top up your balance once and register as many domains as you like.

05How fast is registration?

Under 5 seconds. We debit your balance and call the registry synchronously the moment you click Buy.

06Can I use my own nameservers / Cloudflare?

Yes. You can set custom nameservers at checkout or change them later from your dashboard. Default nameservers point to AnonPort's privacy-respecting DNS.
