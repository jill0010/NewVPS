VPS or dedicated server for a blockchain node?

A VPS is enough for the light chains: a pruned Bitcoin node, Monero, Litecoin, or a Lightning node over a pruned backend all run comfortably on 4–16 GB of RAM and a few hundred GB of NVMe. Our VPS plans go up to 16 vCPU / 64 GB / 1 TB, which covers every chain except the heavyweights.

Ethereum, Solana, TRON and any archival workload want bare metal: dedicated NVMe with real IOPS, ECC memory, and no noisy neighbours during initial sync. Our dedicated servers start at $48.50/mo with 2× 1 TB NVMe and scale to dual EPYC, 256 GB ECC and 16 TB of NVMe — enough for an Ethereum archive node or a Solana validator at the official minimum spec.

Node privacy: Tor, remote RPC and quiet operation

Every chain page includes the flags to run your node over Tor — bitcoind's proxy mode, monerod's tx-proxy, LND's tor.active. Combined with an offshore IP and a no-logs host, the node tells the world very little about you.

Want to use the node from your laptop or phone wallet? Bind RPC to localhost, tunnel over SSH or WireGuard, and your wallet gets sovereign verification without ever touching a public RPC. Our first-hour hardening checklist walks through the firewall side.

FAQ

Node hosting FAQ

01 Can I really rent a node server without any KYC?

Yes. Account creation is a single token — no name, email or phone. You top up in BTC, XMR, ETH, USDT, LTC, TRX or SOL and deploy. That's the entire onboarding.

02 Is bandwidth actually unlimited? Nodes relay a lot.

Actually unlimited on Linux VPS and dedicated servers — no traffic quota, no throttle tiers, no fair-use clause that gets invoked at 2 TB. Nodes are a workload we explicitly welcome; uplinks go up to 10 Gbps on dedicated.

03 Which chains can I run?

Dedicated pages exist for Bitcoin, Lightning, Ethereum, Monero, Solana, Litecoin and TRON. Anything else that runs on Linux — Nostr relays, electrs, BTCPay Server, Tor relays — runs fine too; see crypto node use cases.

04 Do you monitor or interfere with what my node does?

No. We don't log traffic, don't inspect payloads and don't rate-limit P2P ports. Blockchain nodes are legal, welcome workloads across all seven jurisdictions.

05 What happens when the chain outgrows my disk?

Upgrade to a bigger tier from your dashboard; move the data with a snapshot, or resync on the new machine while the old node keeps serving. Every recommendation on these pages already includes 40%+ headroom.

06 Can I stake or run a validator from these servers?

Yes — an Ethereum or Solana validator is just software on your server; keys stay under your control. Validators need excellent uptime: pick dedicated hardware and our 99.9% SLA holds up your side of the schedule.
