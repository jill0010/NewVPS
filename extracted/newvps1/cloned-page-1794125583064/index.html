Why .com

Why a .com is still worth it in 2026

.com is the default the whole world types. It is the oldest and most recognized top-level domain on the internet, and after three decades it still signals legitimacy in a way no newer extension can match. Visitors trust it, remember it, and assume it — type-in traffic and word-of-mouth both quietly favour the .com version of any name.

For a business or a personal brand, a .com improves email deliverability (spam filters trust established TLDs), protects your name on the most valuable extension, and holds far stronger resale value than a discount TLD. For search, a clean, memorable .com earns the clicks and links that actually move rankings.

The only real downside of .com has been price and privacy: mainstream registrars hook you with a cheap first year, then renew at $15.98 and upsell WHOIS protection on top. AnonPort removes both — a cheap $4.99 every year, free FULL WHOIS privacy, and crypto payment with no identity attached. The gold-standard TLD without the gold-standard markup.
