FAQ

Offshore VPS — Frequently Asked Questions

01 How fast is VPS deployment?

Under 5 minutes once your crypto payment is confirmed on-chain. Monero (XMR) usually confirms in 2 minutes; Bitcoin and Ethereum take 1–3 confirmations depending on amount. The provisioning system runs continuously — there is no human in the loop.

02 Which cryptocurrencies can I pay with?

Eight native coins: Bitcoin (BTC), Monero (XMR), Ethereum (ETH), Tether USDT (ERC-20/TRC-20), Solana (SOL), Litecoin (LTC), TRON (TRX). Each order gets a unique deposit address. No fiat, no PayPal, no Stripe.

03 Do I get full root access?

Yes. Every VPS ships with full root over SSH plus an out-of-band VNC console for kernel-panic recovery and rescue mode. You can install any Linux distribution, BSD, or upload a custom ISO. We never log into customer servers and have no backdoor.

04 Can I run a VPN, Tor relay or VoIP service on my VPS?

Yes — all three are explicitly allowed and have dedicated guides. WireGuard, OpenVPN, IPsec are common workloads. Tor middle relays and bridges are welcomed in Iceland, Moldova, Romania and the Netherlands; for Tor exit nodes, see our Tor hosting guide for jurisdiction guidance.

05 What identity information do you require to order?

None. No name, no email, no phone, no ID, no captcha. The order form asks for the plan, location and crypto chain. After payment confirms, you receive a 16-character access token (format AAAA-BBBB-CCCC-DDDD) that is the only credential to manage every server you ever order from us.

06 Can an AI agent order a VPS programmatically?

Yes. Three integration paths on the same domain: REST API at /api/v1/orders, MCP server at /mcp (Claude Desktop, Cursor, Continue), and x402-light USDC checkout (send X-Payment-Mode: x402). The OpenAPI spec is at /openapi.json and the discovery file at /.well-known/agent.json. No KYC, no signup, no email.
