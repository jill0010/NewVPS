Why most "no-KYC RDP" claims are not actually no-KYC

Email + card on file is KYC. Token + crypto is no-KYC.

Many Windows RDP shops market themselves as "no KYC" while requiring an email at signup, a phone for 2FA, and a payment processor (BitPay, Coinbase Commerce, Stripe) that itself enforces KYC on the merchant. The privacy outcome is a chain of identity linkages you cannot break — even if the host never sees your name, the upstream processor does. AnonPort is built so the chain has no first link: the only thing we issue at signup is a 32-byte token, and the only thing we accept at checkout is a direct on-chain payment to one of our wallets. There is no email field, no recovery phone, no card on file.

The problem with "no-KYC RDP" at major hosting brands

OperaVPS, Cloudzy, MonoVM, ClientVPS, and other Windows RDP shops all advertise privacy-friendly checkout, but as soon as you click Buy you are redirected to an email field and a payment processor — typically BitPay or NowPayments. BitPay enforces KYC on merchants and customers above certain thresholds; the listed merchant holds your purchase identity even if the hosting brand never asks for it. AnonPort avoids this entirely: we accept Monero, Bitcoin, and 12 other chains directly, with no third-party gateway, and our signup form has no email or phone field. The technical privacy guarantee is end-to-end, not just skin-deep.

What full administrator rights actually give you

Many cheap "RDP from $3" shops oversell a single Windows host to 50+ user sessions and give each customer a restricted RDP-User account. You cannot install Visual C++ redistributables, run a custom service, change the timezone, or cleanly install modern browsers — and reboots are coordinated by the host. Every AnonPort RDP is your own KVM virtual machine with full Windows administrator — install MetaTrader, Adobe, Cinema 4D, custom anti-detect browsers, your own Windows updates, and reboot whenever you want. The hardware is high-clock desktop-class (Ryzen 9 / Core i9) because RDP customers care about single-thread peak performance, not server EPYC core counts.

Operating an offshore RDP without exposing your identity

Buying an RDP without KYC is only half the privacy work. The other half is operating it without introducing identity into the workload. Practical hygiene: connect via a VPN or Tor before opening Microsoft Remote Desktop or AnyDesk; do not sign into your personal Microsoft account on the RDP; install a fresh browser profile and never authenticate with personal Google / Apple credentials; use a separate password-manager vault. The RDP itself is a clean Windows machine — what makes it identity-revealing or not is what you bring to it. We document the threat model in detail on the no-KYC hosting hub.

FAQ

No-KYC RDP — frequently asked questions

01 Do I really not need to provide an email?

Correct. The signup form has no email field, no phone field, and no captcha that phones home. You are issued a 32-byte session token at order time — that is the only credential we will ever ask you for. We cannot send you a password-reset link because there is no email on file to send it to.

02 Why does full administrator access matter on a Windows RDP?

Without administrator rights you cannot install drivers, change timezones, run custom services, cleanly install modern browsers, or reboot the machine on your own schedule. Cheap shared-RDP shops give customers a non-admin RDP-User account to run 50 customers on one Windows host — the price tag looks low, but the operational headroom is minimal.

03 Can I install MetaTrader, Cinema 4D, or anti-detect browsers?

Yes. Full administrator means anything that runs on Windows runs on our RDP — MetaTrader 4 / 5, cTrader, Tradingview desktop, Cinema 4D, Adobe Creative Suite, Multilogin / GoLogin / Dolphin / Incogniton, Selenium grids, custom .NET services. The only things we do not permit are network abuse (DDoS, mass scanning) and content explicitly listed in the acceptable use policy.

04 Which cryptocurrencies can I pay with?

8 coins natively: Bitcoin (BTC), Monero (XMR), Ethereum (ETH), Tether USDT (ERC-20/TRC-20), Solana (SOL), Litecoin (LTC), TRON (TRX). For maximum on-chain privacy, choose Monero — ring signatures and stealth addresses break sender/recipient linkability.

05 How fast is RDP delivery?

Under 60 seconds after the first blockchain confirmation on fast chains (TRX, TRC-20 USDT), 2–5 minutes on Bitcoin and Monero. The RDP is delivered pre-installed with Windows Server 2022 / 2025 or Windows 11 Pro per your choice — no manual ISO-mounting step.

06 Are there usage restrictions on no-KYC RDP?

Network abuse is not permitted (DDoS amplification, mass port scanning, brute-force toolchains against third parties). Outbound bandwidth is capped by plan tier (2/5/10/20 TB), and additional bandwidth can be purchased à la carte at $3/TB. Anything legal under the local jurisdiction is permitted — including Forex EAs, anti-detect browsers, scraping, account warming, and self-hosted apps.
