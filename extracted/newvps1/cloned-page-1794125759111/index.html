FAQ

About this directory

Why are these providers listed?+

They are the most-searched competitors and adjacent vendors in the anonymous-hosting, offshore-hosting and DMCA-ignored-hosting categories. Some (Njalla, FlokiNET, 1984 Hosting, Incognet, NiceVPS, SporeStack) are direct privacy-first competitors. Others (Hetzner, OVH, AWS, DigitalOcean, Vultr) are mainstream clouds we list as contrast points so you can see what privacy features mainstream pricing trades away.

Are these affiliate links?+

No. The card links go to our internal switching guide for each provider, not to the provider itself. We do not earn referral commissions from any vendor on this page. The pages exist as a neutral resource for users actively comparing options.

How are the providers ranked?+

They are not ranked. The grid is unordered — first-mover icons sit alongside newer entrants and mainstream clouds. Use the filters to surface the subset that matches your criteria (e.g. DMCA-ignored AND accepts Monero AND based in Iceland). For an opinionated head-to-head, open any individual switching guide and see the feature-by-feature breakdown there.

Can I suggest a provider that is missing?+

Yes. Send the provider name, public website and a one-line description through our contact page. We add candidates that have a real privacy proposition (no-KYC, crypto billing, offshore jurisdiction, or a documented DMCA stance) and skip resellers, dropshippers and shell brands.
